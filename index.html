About NEXUS

Founded in 2010, NEXUS is a generative design studio dedicated to exploring the intersection of art, technology, and computation. We specialize in creating algorithmic systems that transcend traditional design paradigms.

Our practice is rooted in the belief that meaningful creative work emerges from the collaboration between intentional design and computational autonomy. We create systems that surprise their creators.
